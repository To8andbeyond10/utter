Questions

Where does it work?

It's a browser extension for desktop Chrome and Edge.

Does ClariFi see what I browse?

Checks run on your device. ClariFi never sends the pages you visit or the posts you see anywhere, and it doesn't keep your browsing history.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as its evidence, and it tells you which kind it has. "Labeled by YouTube" is a fact. "Likely AI" is an estimate, and the card says so.

Will it hide real posts?

Block only acts on confirmed or strong evidence unless you choose otherwise. You can always show a hidden post with one tap.
