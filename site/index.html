Questions

Where does it work?

First as an extension for Chrome, Edge and Firefox on Windows. The Android app follows and works inside the YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X apps.

Does ClariFi see what I browse?

Checks run on your device. Nothing leaves it unless you run a Deep Scan on a specific post, and we don't keep your browsing history.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as its evidence, and it tells you which kind it has. "Labeled by YouTube" is a fact. "Likely AI, 88%" is an estimate, and the card says so.

Will it hide real posts?

Block only acts on confirmed or strong evidence unless you choose otherwise. You can always show a hidden post with one tap.
