Privacy

Last updated September 28, 2026.

What the waitlist collects

Your email address, the three answers you pick on the form, and the date you joined. Nothing else.

How it's used

To email you when Clearband is ready and to decide what to build first. We don't sell or share your email.

Where it's stored

In private storage on Vercel, the service that hosts this site. Only the Clearband team can read it.

Leaving the list

Reply to any email from us and ask to be removed. Your record is deleted within 7 days.

The Clearband app

When the app launches, its checks run on your device. It won't send what you browse anywhere unless you choose to run a Deep Scan on a specific post. The app will have its own privacy policy with full details.
